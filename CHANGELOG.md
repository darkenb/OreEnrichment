# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Six ore dusts, made by crushing in an assembler (1 ore -> 1 dust) or washing in a chemical plant (2 ore + 1 water -> 3 dust).
- Enriched smelting for iron, magnets, copper, stone, glass, graphite, silicon, titanium and steel in three tiers: 1.5, 2 and 3 times the products of the vanilla recipe.
- Catalysed copies of every other material, component, ammunition and matrix recipe: three batches of inputs and one catalyst give four batches of products, in the same building; five technologies and "Catalysis" tabs.
- Gold and silver from copper refining (anode slime): x1.5 / x2 / x3 recipes for the electronic parts, and a gold or silver copy of every other recipe that uses them, components x2 (x2.5 with both metals) and buildings x1.5 (x2); two "Au-Ag" tabs and three technologies.
- With GenesisBook: nitric acid parting, Wohlwill gold refining, fluid smelting of dust (x2.5) and seawater washing, under a "Fluid Chemistry" technology.
- Simple recipes can be made in the replicator, as their normal recipe; recipes with fluids, chemical-plant recipes and catalysed copies cannot.
- Texts in English, Chinese and Turkish (`Language` setting).
- Four technologies and an "Enrichment" tab for the new recipes, spread over assembler, smelter, chemical plant, refinery and particle collider.
